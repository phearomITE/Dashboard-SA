# Cambodia administrative boundaries

Source: Cambodia Department of Geography, Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction. Published by the OCHA Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific as Cambodia COD-AB, version 01, valid from 4 October 2018. [Dataset and metadata](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-khm).

The bundled file uses the province and commune layers from the GeoJSON archive. It retains their English names, codes and centers, rounds coordinates to five decimal places and compresses the resulting JSON. The data contains 25 provinces and 1,633 communes. Administrative names and boundaries reflect this edition.
